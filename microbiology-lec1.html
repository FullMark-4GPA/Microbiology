🌍 Discover the Fascinating World of Microbes

🔬 Medical Microbiology — Definition
  • •The science which is concerned with the study of microbes and the interaction between microbes and hosts leading to infectious diseases.
  • •Microbes are living organisms that can only be seen by microscopes and they are sometimes called microorganisms although viruses are not true organisms.

Morphology of Bacteria

Size · Gram staining · Shape · Arrangement

📏 Size of Bacterial Cell
  • •The size of the bacterial cell is measured by micron (micron = 1/1000 mm).
  • •So, they cannot be seen by the naked eye — they are seen using an Ordinary Light Microscope.
🎨 Gram Staining
  • •Bacteria are stained with Gram stain that can differentiate bacteria into:
  • ➢Gram positive (bacteria appearing violet), or
  • ➢Gram negative (bacteria appearing pink).
🔷 Shape of the Bacterial Cell
  • aCocci — e.g. Staphylococci
  • bBacilli — e.g. Diphtheria
  • cSpiral
  • dCurved or comma shaped
🔗 Arrangement of Bacteria
  • •Single — as Gram negative bacilli (E. coli)
  • •Pairs — as Neisseria and pneumococci
  • •Chains — as streptococci, Anthrax bacilli
  • •Grape like — as staphylococci
  • •Palisade(متراصة كالسياج) — as Diphtheria bacilli

Bacterial Structure

Cell envelope · Cytoplasmic components · Cell appendages · Spores

🧱 The Basic Components of Bacterial Cells
  • ACell envelope: Cell wall · Cytoplasmic membrane · Capsule.
  • BCytoplasmic components: Nuclear body · Plasmid · Ribosomes.
  • CCell appendages: Flagellae · Fimbriae "Pili".
(1) Cell Wall
  • •It is the layer that lies outside the cytoplasmic membrane.
  • •All bacterial cells: the cell wall is composed of "peptidoglycan".
  • •The peptidoglycan layer is present in both Gram positive & negative bacteria.
⚖️ Differences Between Gram Positive & Gram-Negative Bacterial Cell Wall
Gram positiveGram negative
PeptidoglycanThick · Up to 40 sheetsVery thin · 1 or 2 sheets
Special componentsTeichoic acidLipoprotein · Outer membrane · Periplasmic space(الحيز حول البلازمي) · Lipopolysaccharides (lipid A = Endotoxin(السم الداخلي) · Polysaccharide = somatic antigen(المستضد الجسمي O))
🛡️ Functions of the Cell Wall
  • •Preservation of the shape of the cell.
  • •Protective function.
  • •Antigenic(مستضدي) character: Teichoic acid in G+ve (major surface Ag) · Lipopolysaccharide in G–ve (major surface O Ag).
  • •Staining reaction.
  • •Toxicity.
  • •Target action of antibiotics: as penicillin and cephalosporins.
(2) Cytoplasmic Membrane
Def: It is a thin elastic membrane that lies immediately under the cell wall.
Nature: protein and phospholipids.
Functions
  • •Transport and permeability(النفاذية) function.
  • •Excretion of hydrolytic enzymes(إنزيمات محللة) and toxins.
  • •Chemotactic(الانجذاب الكيميائي) function.
  • •Energy Production.
(3) Capsule
Def: It is a gelatinous(هلامية) material outside the cell wall, produced by some pathogenic bacteria.
Chemical structure
  • •All bacteria are complex polysaccharides.
  • •In B. anthracis → polypeptide.
Functions
  • ➢Protect bacterial cell against phagocytosis(البلعمة).
  • ➢Determining virulence(الضراوة) of the organism.
  • ➢Antigenic(مستضدي).
B) Cytoplasmic Components — (1) Ribosomes
  • •The factory of protein synthesis in the cell.
Bacterial ribosomes consist of (70S): Large subunit: 50S · Small subunit: 30S. S value = Svedberg unit.
(2) Nuclear Body (Nucleoid(المنطقة النووية))
  • •No nuclear membrane.
  • •DNA molecule folded on itself.
  • •Single chromosome is double stranded circular DNA — carries genetic characters.
(3) Plasmids
  • •Extrachromosomal(خارج الكروموسوم) genetic elements.
  • •Circular double stranded DNA.
  • •Carry certain function: antibiotic resistance genes.
C) Cell Appendages — (1) Flagella
Def: long helical filament(خيط حلزوني).
Structure: contractile protein(بروتين انقباضي) called flagellin.
Functions
  • 1Motility.
  • 2Antigenicity(خاصية المستضدية): H antigen.
(2) Fimbriae (Pili)
  • •They are short, hair like filaments.
  • •They are shorter, thinner, more numerous than flagella, straight.
  • •They are formed of protein "pilin".
Functions
  • •Ordinary pili — adhesion(الالتصاق) to host cells.
  • •Sex pili — "conjugation(الاقتران البكتيري)".
  • •Virulence(الضراوة).
🌱 Bacterial Spores
Definition: Some bacteria are capable of forming resistant endospores(الأبواغ الداخلية) in vitro(خارج الجسم / معمليًا) (outside the body) under certain unfavorable conditions as starvation.
Function: They are highly resistant forms.
Examples of Spore Forming Organisms
  • •Clostridium
  • •Bacillus

⭐ Key Points

أهم النقاط في المحاضرة — الحاجات اللي بتتكرر في الامتحان
1
Medical microbiology
The science concerned with the study of microbes and the interaction between microbes and hosts leading to infectious diseases.
2
Microbes
Living organisms seen only by microscopes; viruses are not true organisms.
3
4 classes of pathogens
Bacteria (Prokaryotic(بدائية النواة)) · Fungi (Eukaryotic(حقيقية النواة)) · Viruses (Acellular(غير خلوية)) · Parasites (Eukaryotic).
4
Prokaryotes(بدائية النواة) vs Eukaryotes(حقيقية النواة)
Prokaryotes: no nuclear membrane, no membrane-bound organelles, haploid(أحادي الصيغة الصبغية), 70S ribosome, peptidoglycan present.
5
Bacterial cell size
Measured in micron (1/1000 mm) — cannot be seen by the naked eye; seen with an Ordinary Light Microscope.
6
Gram stain
Gram positive = appears violet. Gram negative = appears pink.
7
Bacterial shapes
Cocci (e.g. Staphylococci) · Bacilli (e.g. Diphtheria) · Spiral · Curved/comma shaped.
8
Arrangement
Single (E. coli) · Pairs (Neisseria, pneumococci) · Chains (streptococci, Anthrax bacilli) · Grape like (staphylococci) · Palisade(متراصة كالسياج) (Diphtheria bacilli).
9
Cell wall composition
All bacterial cell walls contain "peptidoglycan", present in both Gram positive & negative bacteria.
10
Peptidoglycan thickness
Gram positive: thick, up to 40 sheets. Gram negative: very thin, 1–2 sheets.
11
Special cell wall components
Gram +ve: Teichoic acid. Gram –ve: Lipoprotein, Outer membrane, Periplasmic space(الحيز حول البلازمي), Lipopolysaccharides (lipid A = Endotoxin(السم الداخلي); polysaccharide = somatic antigen(المستضد الجسمي O)).
12
Cell wall functions
Shape preservation · Protection · Antigenic(مستضدي) character · Staining reaction · Toxicity · Target of penicillin & cephalosporins.
13
Cytoplasmic membrane
Thin elastic membrane under the cell wall; nature = protein & phospholipids. Functions: transport/permeability(النفاذية), excretion of enzymes/toxins, chemotactic(الانجذاب الكيميائي), energy production.
14
Capsule
Gelatinous(هلامية) material outside the cell wall; complex polysaccharide (except B. anthracis = polypeptide). Protects against phagocytosis(البلعمة), determines virulence(الضراوة), is antigenic(مستضدي).
15
Ribosomes
Factory of protein synthesis. Bacterial = 70S (Large subunit 50S + Small subunit 30S). S = Svedberg unit.
16
Nuclear body
No nuclear membrane; DNA folded on itself; single chromosome = double stranded circular DNA.
17
Plasmids
Extrachromosomal(خارج الكروموسوم), circular double stranded DNA; carry antibiotic resistance genes.
18
Flagella
Long helical filament(خيط حلزوني); structure = contractile protein(بروتين انقباضي) "flagellin". Functions: motility, antigenicity(خاصية المستضدية) (H antigen).
19
Fimbriae (Pili)
Short, hair-like, straight; formed of protein "pilin". Ordinary pili = adhesion(الالتصاق); Sex pili = conjugation(الاقتران البكتيري); also linked to virulence(الضراوة).
20
Bacterial spores
Resistant endospores(الأبواغ الداخلية) formed under unfavorable conditions (e.g. starvation); highly resistant forms. Examples: Clostridium, Bacillus.

🧬 Structural Atlas

كل مكوّن في الخلية البكتيرية — تعريفه، تركيبه، ووظيفته في كارت واحد. ابحث بالاسم أو اختار مجموعة.

🧱 Cell wall

A) Cell envelope
Definition
The layer that lies outside the cytoplasmic membrane.
Chemical structure
Composed of "peptidoglycan" in all bacterial cells — present in both Gram +ve & Gram –ve.
Gram +ve vs –ve
G+ve: thick, up to 40 sheets, has Teichoic acid. G–ve: very thin (1–2 sheets), has Lipoprotein, Outer membrane, Periplasmic space(الحيز حول البلازمي), Lipopolysaccharides.
Functions
Shape preservation · Protection · Antigenic(مستضدي) character · Staining reaction · Toxicity · Target of penicillin & cephalosporins.

💧 Cytoplasmic membrane

A) Cell envelope
Definition
A thin elastic membrane that lies immediately under the cell wall.
Nature
Protein and phospholipids.
Functions
Transport & permeability(النفاذية) · Excretion of hydrolytic enzymes(إنزيمات محللة) and toxins · Chemotactic(الانجذاب الكيميائي) function · Energy production.

🫧 Capsule

A) Cell envelope
Definition
A gelatinous(هلامية) material outside the cell wall, produced by some pathogenic bacteria.
Chemical structure
All bacteria: complex polysaccharides. In B. anthracis → polypeptide.
Functions
Protects against phagocytosis(البلعمة) · Determines virulence(الضراوة) · Antigenic(مستضدي).

⚙️ Ribosomes

B) Cytoplasmic components
Definition
The factory of protein synthesis in the cell.
Structure
Bacterial ribosomes = 70S — Large subunit 50S + Small subunit 30S. S value = Svedberg unit.

🧬 Nuclear body (Nucleoid(المنطقة النووية))

B) Cytoplasmic components
Key facts
No nuclear membrane · DNA molecule folded on itself · Single chromosome = double stranded circular DNA carrying genetic characters.

🔵 Plasmids

B) Cytoplasmic components
Key facts
Extrachromosomal(خارج الكروموسوم) genetic elements · Circular double stranded DNA · Carry certain function: antibiotic resistance genes.

🌀 Flagella

C) Cell appendages
Definition
Long helical filament(خيط حلزوني).
Structure
Contractile protein(بروتين انقباضي) called flagellin.
Functions
1) Motility · 2) Antigenicity(خاصية المستضدية): H antigen.

🧵 Fimbriae (Pili)

C) Cell appendages
Definition
Short, hair like filaments — shorter, thinner, more numerous than flagella, straight.
Structure
Formed of protein "pilin".
Functions
Ordinary pili — adhesion(الالتصاق) to host cells · Sex pili — "conjugation(الاقتران البكتيري)" · Virulence(الضراوة).

🌱 Bacterial spores (Endospores(الأبواغ الداخلية))

Resistant form
Definition
Some bacteria form resistant endospores in vitro(خارج الجسم / معمليًا) under unfavorable conditions as starvation.
Function
Highly resistant forms.
Examples
Clostridium · Bacillus.
